# Data Sources and References

## 1. European Social Survey

**Dataset:** European Social Survey (ESS), Round 11 Integrated Dataset, Edition 4.2 (2023/24).

**Official data portal:** https://www.europeansocialsurvey.org/data-portal

**Purpose:** The dataset provides survey responses used to compare political interest, voting behaviour, trust in politicians, contacting politicians, and online political activity across age groups.

## 2. Analysis Tools

- SQL — data queries and descriptive analysis
